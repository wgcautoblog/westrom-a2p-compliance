SMS opt-in is completely optional. You can lease a property, create or keep an account, pay rent, request maintenance, complete any transaction, or receive any Westrom Group property-management service without consenting to SMS. Messaging consent is never bundled with a lease, onboarding paperwork, or required service terms.

If you choose to opt in, you do so only by: (a) visiting our public SMS Opt-In page, entering your mobile number, and checking an unchecked-by-default optional consent box; or (b) texting START or YES to +1 (469) 888-8560.
